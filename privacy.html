Indian Movie Quiz · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Overview

Indian Movie Quiz is a cinema trivia and party game. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use the app. Some features work locally on your device, while app stores and integrated third-party services may process information needed to provide purchases, diagnostics or advertising.

Data used by the app

Local game data: game preferences, chosen player names, scores and progress may be stored on your device to support gameplay and resume sessions. Do not enter sensitive personal information as player names.

Purchases: optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Purchase-related product and transaction identifiers may be used to validate access and restore purchases. We do not ask you to provide payment card details inside the game.

Technical and service data: the app integrates Google Firebase services and may use associated technical identifiers and service logs for supporting app functions, security and troubleshooting. The data involved depends on which Firebase features are enabled in the released app.

Advertising: the app includes or may include Google advertising services. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, ad interactions and other information according to applicable consent choices and their policies. Premium may remove ads from a purchased cinema collection.

Purposes and legal basis

Data may be processed to operate gameplay, remember local choices, deliver and verify purchased features, secure the service, troubleshoot issues and, where enabled and permitted, show advertising. Where consent is required for personalized advertising or device tracking, it should be requested using the relevant platform controls.

Sharing and service providers

Depending on platform and enabled features, information may be processed by Apple, Google Play, Firebase or Google advertising services. Their own privacy notices govern how they handle information as independent providers or service providers, as applicable.

Retention and choices

Local game data generally remains on your device until you clear it or remove the app, subject to device backup behavior. Purchase records may be retained by the app store and applicable verification services for transaction, compliance and fraud-prevention purposes. You can manage permissions and advertising preferences through device settings.

To request access, correction or deletion of information we control, email indian.movie.quiz@gmail.com. We may need sufficient information to locate your request without collecting unnecessary sensitive data.

Children and international users

The app's suitability for children depends on its final age rating and enabled advertising settings. Parents and guardians should review the app's content and device controls. Providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable safeguards.
