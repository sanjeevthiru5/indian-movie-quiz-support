Support & help centre

How can we help?

Indian Movie Quiz brings together Tamil, Malayalam, Hindi, Telugu and Kannada cinema quizzes and party challenges.

For help with the app, report a problem or request assistance, contact us at indian.movie.quiz@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Choose a cinema collection, a game mode and your preferred settings, then tap Start playing.

How do Premium purchases work?

Each cinema has a separate optional one-time Premium purchase. The price and availability are displayed in the app before purchase. Payment is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play, depending on your device.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the relevant cinema collection, open its Premium panel and select Restore purchases. Use the same store account that made the original purchase. If the purchase does not reappear, contact support with your device type and the cinema collection; do not send passwords or full payment details.

Why can't I access Premium?

Check that the app is up to date and that you are signed in to the appropriate store account. Try Restore purchases, then contact support if the issue persists.

Can I play offline?

Quiz content can be played offline once it is available on your device. Store purchases, restoration and some optional services may require a connection.

How do I report an incorrect question?

Email the cinema collection, question text and suggested correction to our support address.
